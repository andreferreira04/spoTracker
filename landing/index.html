Before you install

Windows Defender flagged it. Is it safe?

Small apps without a paid code-signing certificate are often flagged by Windows. The code is public on GitHub, so you can read exactly what it does before you install it.

Why not just wait for Wrapped?

Wrapped arrives once a year and shows what Spotify chooses to show. SpoTracker updates your charts every day, and you decide what to look at.

Do I have to log in to Spotify?

Not to track. Connecting your Spotify account is optional and only adds playlist charts and artist pages.

Will it slow down my PC?

No. It is a small background process that uses almost no CPU or memory.

Why I built this

Every December I opened Spotify Wrapped, took a screenshot and forgot about it. Months later I would find it in my gallery and wonder what I had actually been listening to since.

Wrapped is fun, but it comes once a year and only shows what Spotify decides to show. I wanted real numbers, every day.

So I built SpoTracker: a small app that runs on my PC, keeps my listening history locally and turns it into charts I actually want to look at.

It is free and open source. If it is useful to you too, that is the best outcome I could ask for.
