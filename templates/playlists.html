Your Spotify playlists, ranked by how much you actually listen to their songs.

Connect Spotify to see your playlists

This page reads your playlists from Spotify and matches them with the listening history SpoTracker has recorded. Once you log in you'll see:

  • Every playlist you own or follow, plus your Liked Songs, ranked by the time you spent on their songs
  • Each playlist's songs as a chart, with your plays, listening time and when you last played them
  • Songs you play a lot that aren't in any playlist, favourites you stopped playing, hidden gems, and songs you added but never played

Your playlists are read through SpoTracker on this computer; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
