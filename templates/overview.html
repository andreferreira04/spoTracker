You can bring in years of past listening by importing your full Spotify history. Plays {{ app_name }} already recorded are not counted twice.

  1. Open Spotify → Account → Privacy settings and sign in.
  2. Under Download your data, select Extended streaming history and click Request data.
  3. Confirm the request in the email Spotify sends you.
  4. Wait for the email with your data (Spotify says it can take up to 30 days, usually less) and download the .zip.
  5. In {{ app_name }}, open Settings from the tray icon menu, click Import Spotify history (.zip)… and pick the file.
